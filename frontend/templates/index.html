Evaluated Model Paradigms & Architecture

Identical data partitions, feature transformers, and threshold tuning protocols
● Classical ML
scikit-learn

HistGradientBoosting

Histogram-based gradient boosted decision trees with balanced sample weights. Hyperparameters selected via 5-fold cross-validation on training data only.

■ Deep Learning
PyTorch 2.4

PyTorch Feed-Forward MLP

Multi-layer perceptron with hidden layers [64, 32], dropout (0.3), Adam optimizer, positive class weighting, and early stopping on validation loss.

◆ Quantum ML
PennyLane 0.38

PennyLane VQC Simulator

8-qubit variational quantum classifier on default.qubit simulator with angle embedding in [0, π], strongly entangling layers, and classical readout.
